# Trauma-Informed Abuse Resources

## Goal
Create a calm, practical resources page for people experiencing image-based abuse, domestic violence, sexual violence, stalking, sextortion, or related crises, covering the United States, Canada, and the European Union where services apply.

## What will change
- Add a public **Abuse support resources** page with a prominent emergency section, safer-device/privacy guidance, and reassuring trauma-informed language.
- Organize verified support into U.S., Canada, and EU sections, separating crisis help, confidential support, image-removal tools, and directories for nearby organizations.
- Make phone, text, chat, and official-site actions easy to identify without forcing visitors to disclose information to Hygi.
- Add a direct support callout inside **When an Intimate Image Is Shared Without Consent**.
- Add the page to the site footer and sitemap.
- Verify links, keyboard behavior, screen-reader structure, desktop/mobile layout, and page metadata.

## Safety principles
- State clearly that the abuse is not the visitor’s fault and they control what happens next.
- Warn that browsing history and device activity may be visible to an abuser; offer a neutral quick-exit destination without making false promises that history is erased.
- Distinguish immediate danger from emotional crisis and non-emergency support.
- Note regional limitations and that hotline availability can change.

## Technical details
- Use a dedicated TanStack route with unique title, description, Open Graph, Twitter, and canonical metadata.
- Use existing semantic colors, typography, buttons, and accessible focus styles.
- Use only official or primary service URLs and current phone/text details verified against source organizations.
