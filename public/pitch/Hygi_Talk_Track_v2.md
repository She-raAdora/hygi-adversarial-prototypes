## 1. Title — "Unique. Layered. Recoverable."
"Hygi turns published university and federal security guidance into short lessons people actually
finish. It's live at digitalhygiene.app, brought to you by NorthBridge — and the account-security
curriculum was just rebuilt on the current NIST and CISA guidance."

- Open the app on screen if you can. The homepage does the pitch faster than a slide.
- "Unique. Layered. Recoverable." is the spine of all account-security teaching in the app.

## 2. The problem
"Nobody lacks security advice. They lack a version they'll read to the end — and a version that
isn't out of date."

- Compliance training is long, jargon-heavy, and measured by a completion checkbox.
- Most password advice still circulating (90-day rotations, complexity rules, "SMS is strong")
  contradicts current federal guidance. Being finishable isn't enough; it also has to be correct.

## 3. The insight
"Advice becomes habit in five-minute pieces with immediate proof you understood it."

- Short session, plain language, one mini-quiz, one badge. That loop is the whole product.
- Every answer gets an explainer plus a "Review this lesson" link straight back to the section.

## 4. The product
"22 lessons across 7 pathways, 73 quiz questions, 78 glossary terms — and a trophy when all 22 are
done."

- Any lesson, any order: Foundations · Accounts & Identity · Scams & Social Engineering · Devices &
  Workspaces · Home Network & Data · Privacy & Footprint · Safety & Response.
- Jargon is tappable for a plain-language definition, backed by the full 78-term Digital Safety
  Glossary.
- New: the **Five-Door Reset** — pick your five highest-stakes accounts and harden each one
  (unique passphrase, passkey or app-based MFA, recovery codes, session review).
- New: **"Evidence behind this lesson"** — an expandable panel linking the exact source behind the
  guidance.

## 5. What's new since v1
"Same five-minute format, modernized content, and a citation trail."

- **Protect Your Accounts** rewritten: one lock per door, 15+ character passphrases, no composition
  rules, no calendar rotation, passkeys taught first, tiered MFA ladder
  (passkey > security key > authenticator app > push > SMS).
- **Lock Down Account Access** is now a dedicated recovery unit: recovery codes, active sessions,
  connected apps, SIM-swap risk, verification-code safety.
- **Audit Your Digital Footprint** reframes breach checkers as a smoke detector — a useful warning,
  not proof of safety.
- Hygi Helper is hardened with explicit rules so it can't repeat obsolete password advice.

## 6. Why it works
"Four things do the work: short sessions, retrieval practice, plain language, and a visible reason
to come back."

- Quizzes aren't scoring — they're retrieval. Answer, then read why the right answer is right.
- Badges and the trophy convert scattered reading into a streak worth finishing. Sharing a result
  card is always optional and never unlocks anything.

## 7. Credibility
"Adapted from NIST, CISA, Dartmouth, Caltech, Cal Poly, and Harvard — every source cited in the
app."

- Primary authorities: NIST SP 800-63B-4 (July 2025) and CISA (Secure Our World, Implementing
  Phishing-Resistant MFA, Project Upskill for high-risk communities).
- University guidance: Dartmouth's Guide to Digital Hygiene, Caltech IMSS Weeks 2–4, Cal Poly's Top
  10 Security Practices, Harvard T.H. Chan's Digital Safety Kit.
- Sources are listed per lesson, at the trophy, and on the pillar pages. Hygi is not affiliated with
  or endorsed by these institutions. We changed the format, not the recommendations.

## 8. Who it's for
"Three audiences, one product: universities and IT helpdesks, small businesses and nonprofits, and
individuals and families."

- Universities: their own guidance, in a format students and staff complete.
- SMBs and nonprofits: awareness training with no LMS and no budget line.
- Individuals: free, installable, plain language, no account needed to learn.

- Referring-domain attribution ties backlink gains to onboarding and quiz completion.
- Opt-in only, anonymous and aggregate, progress stored on the learner's device.

## Anticipated questions

**How is this different from existing awareness training?**
Those tools optimize for audit evidence. Hygi optimizes for completion and comprehension, and
reports both.

**Is the content current?**
Yes — that's the point of this refresh. Account security follows NIST SP 800-63B-4 (July 2025) and
CISA's phishing-resistant MFA guidance: length over complexity, no forced rotation, passkeys first,
SMS as a last resort. Each lesson shows the source it came from.

**Why does the password advice differ from what our IT policy says?**
If your policy still forces 90-day rotations or complexity rules, Hygi will say NIST retired that
guidance. We can flag that tension in a pilot so your policy and training say the same thing.

**Is the content trustworthy?**
It's adapted from published NIST, CISA, Dartmouth, Caltech, Cal Poly, and Harvard guidance, cited
in-app per lesson. We changed the format, not the recommendations.

**What data do you collect?**
Progress is stored on the learner's device. Analytics are anonymous, aggregate, and off until
someone opts in. Privacy policy, terms, accessibility statement, and an account-deletion path are
all in the app.

**Can we brand it or run it for our org?**
That's the roadmap: white-label curriculum, cohorts, and certificates. A pilot is the fastest way to
shape it.

**Why should learners care after week one?**
Badges, the trophy at 22, shareable result cards, and the Five-Door Reset, which gives people a
concrete task on their own accounts instead of more reading.
